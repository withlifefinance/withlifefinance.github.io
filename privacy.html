Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

WithLife Finance ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal finance tracker designed to help you manage transactions, recurring payments, savings goals, and borrower ledgers.

1. Local-first data storage and Google Drive backup

All your financial data (transactions, recurring payments, contacts, and borrower ledgers) is stored locally on your device first (offline-first).

To enable backup and restore across your devices, the app lets you back up your data to your personal Google Drive account. Your data is saved in a private, app-restricted folder ("appDataFolder") and sent over HTTPS/TLS.

2. Google Drive data usage and scopes

The app requests the Google Drive AppData scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) solely to create, update, and restore your backup file (withlife_finance_backup.json) in your personal Google Drive.

  • We do not read, access, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • We do not store your financial data on our own servers or third-party databases.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, and approximate location to show and measure ads. Your financial data, such as transactions, ledgers and notes, is not shared with AdMob or advertisers.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps.

4. No selling of your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data. Apart from the advertising data described in section 3, we do not share your data with third parties, and it is not used for artificial intelligence or machine learning training.

5. Data retention and user control

  • You can delete individual transactions, contacts, or ledger entries at any time inside the app.
  • You can delete your Google Drive backup file at any time from Settings > Delete Google Drive Backup in the app.
  • You can revoke Google Drive permissions at any time in your Google Account permissions.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
