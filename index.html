WithLife Finance

Track every rupee without opening the app. A simple, private personal finance manager with home-screen widgets, reminders, a borrower and lender ledger, savings goals and multi-device sync.

Get it on Google Play

Add an expense in two taps

The Quick Add widget puts your most-used categories on your home screen. Tap Food, type the amount, save. The Overview widget shows your balance, today's and this month's spending, and your next payment due.

Track your money

  • Log income, expenses and deposits with custom categories
  • Dashboard with spending breakdowns, monthly summary and charts
  • Spending calendar and detailed reports
  • Recurring entries for rent, salary, SIPs and EMIs
  • Upcoming payments with reminders

Borrow and lend ledger

Track money given and taken, with return dates and settled status. Send a payment request as a UPI QR code to any app, including WhatsApp.

Savings goals

Set a goal, target amount and timeline. See how much to save each month and watch your progress bar fill.

Handy tools

EMI calculator, SIP and FD calculator, loan prepayment, GST, discount and tip, bill split, emergency fund, net worth, trip planner, fuel log, subscriptions tracker, expiry and medicine reminders, secure notes and more.

Backup and sync

Sync across your devices with a Sync Code, back up to your phone's storage, and export your data to Excel and import it back. You can delete your data any time from inside the app.
